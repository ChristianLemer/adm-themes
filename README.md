# Themes for Administratum

A pack of themes for [Administratum](https://github.com/Arylmera/Administratum), the pixel-art hall that shows your
Claude Code sessions. A pack is data only: colours, wording and sprite sheets. It cannot run code.

## What is in it

| Folder | World | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `pelion/` | Pelion | The school of Chiron on Mount Pelion: students in chiton and himation at marble tables, a stoa open on the Aegean. Work in progress: the students, the room, the tables, the wall furniture, Chiron and his place, the mechanism, the owl, the propylon and the fires are drawn; the small props, the side rooms and the 2.5D view still show the default art |
| `breakingbad/` | Breaking Bad | The superlab under the laundry, drawn flat: one outline colour and flat fills, cooks in hazmat suits, the Br Ba tiles as the one decoration. Its own art for every family |

## Install

In Administratum: **Settings > Hall > Added themes**, type

```
ChristianLemer/adm-themes
```

then *Install*, and restart the app. The world then appears in the *Theme* picker.

To make your own pack, see Administratum's guide, `docs/theme-packs.md`.

`generators/` holds the scripts some of the sheets were drawn with; see its README. It is not part of the pack.
